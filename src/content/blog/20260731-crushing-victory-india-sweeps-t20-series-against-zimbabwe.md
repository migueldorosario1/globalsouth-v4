India clinched victory in the second Twenty20 (T20) international against Zimbabwe, taking a 2-0 lead in the series. According to aljazeera.com, the match was held at Harare Sports Club, where India emerged victorious by a substantial margin of 90 runs. 

Ishan Kishan and Tilak Varma were standout performers, contributing significantly to India's formidable total. Kishan scored 81 runs off 44 balls, while Varma hit an unbeaten 60 off 29, helping India amass a total of 219-5 after being put into bat. Their partnership of 94 runs off 44 balls was instrumental in setting a challenging target for Zimbabwe. 

Zimbabwe's response never reached the heights set by India. Opener Brian Bennett showed some fight with a score of 32 off 19 balls, but was eventually dismissed by Yash Thakur, who ended with figures of 2-30 in his T20 international debut. Zimbabwe was ultimately bowled out for 129 in 17.5 overs, failing to mount a serious challenge to India's total. 

Abhishek Sharma's slow left-arm bowling was particularly effective, yielding the best figures of 3-17 for India. The previous match in the series, also at Harare Sports Club, saw India win by seven wickets with 40 balls to spare. The series finale is set for Sunday. 

India's dominant performance underlines their strength in this format and their ability to secure series wins on the road, which is a testament to their cricketing prowess and the depth of their squad.

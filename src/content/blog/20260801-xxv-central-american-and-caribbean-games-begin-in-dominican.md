The XXV Central American and Caribbean Games Santo Domingo 2026 were officially inaugurated on this Friday, marking a milestone as the event celebrates 100 years of history. The opening ceremony took place at the Félix Sánchez Olympic Stadium, which was filled to its maximum capacity with 24,500 spectators.

In an unprecedented technological show, organizers used over 1,100 drones to light up the night sky, along with 30,000 LED devices spread across the stands, creating a spectacle that highlighted both the technological prowess and the spirit of the games.

The games, which run from July 24 to August 8, will see 6,200 athletes from 37 countries compete for medals across 40 sports and 53 disciplines. Following the opening ceremony, the traditional parade of participating national delegations started, with Mexico taking the lead in the procession.

The XXV edition of the Central American and Caribbean Games not only serves as a platform for athletic excellence but also as a celebration of the rich sporting history of the region and its commitment to fostering unity and camaraderie among nations through sports. As the games unfold, they will undoubtedly provide a stage for athletes to showcase their talents and for nations to demonstrate their support for the spirit of friendly competition.

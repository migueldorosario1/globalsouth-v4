<p>The West's foreign policy structure is undergoing a profound crisis, marked by the failure of proxy conflicts and economic sanctions. In a detailed discussion on the emerging multipolar world order, political and legal analyst Robert Barnes explained how unilateral Western dominance is rapidly deteriorating. He argued that the ongoing conflict in Ukraine is serving as a catalyst for a global realignment, pushing key Eastern powers into closer security and economic cooperation, effectively leaving the United States and its European allies isolated in their strategic ambitions.</p><p>Addressing the current state of the war, Robert Barnes asserted that Ukraine is on the verge of a systemic collapse on the front lines. He highlighted that despite continuous Western funding and military aid, Kiev is facing severe structural issues, including widespread corruption and a critical shortage of personnel. According to Barnes, the conflict is moving toward a decisive Russian victory, which will likely result in Ukraine losing control over all territories east of the Dnieper River, as well as crucial Black Sea ports like Odessa, leaving only a small, embarrassed rump state in the west.</p><p>The analyst criticized the Western establishment for underestimating Russia's economic and military self-sufficiency. In the interview on the channel Daniel Davis / Deep Dive, conducted by presenter Daniel Davis, Robert Barnes pointed out that despite unprecedented Western sanctions, the Russian economy remains remarkably strong, with rising real wages and growing domestic production. He contrasted this resilience with the self-inflicted deindustrialization of Europe, particularly Germany, which is now facing severe energy crises and public backlash against its political elites.</p><p>Robert Barnes explained that the aggressive sanctions regime has completely backfired, failing to isolate targeted nations. Instead of weakening adversaries, Western policies have pushed Russia, China, and Iran into a tightly-knit, self-sufficient tripartite alliance. Barnes observed that the United States' strategy of rapprochement with Russia to isolate China has been completely derailed. This failure, he argued, is leading to the creation of a powerful Eurasian coalition that can bypass Western financial systems entirely, further undermining the unipolar dominance of the US dollar.</p><p>Drawing historical parallels, Robert Barnes compared the current Western leadership to the incompetent elite classes that led their nations into World War I. He argued that just as the British, Ottoman, Austro-Hungarian, and Russian empires collapsed due to moral decay and strategic ineptitude a century ago, the modern neoliberal and neoconservative establishment is steering the Western world toward a similar historic decline. Barnes emphasized that the only factor preventing a catastrophic global war is the strategic restraint exercised by leaders in Moscow, Beijing, and Tehran.</p><p>Finally, Robert Barnes addressed the political shift occurring within Western societies as a direct result of these failed foreign policies. He noted that the European public is rapidly turning against its ruling class, as evidenced by the dramatic rise of populist movements in France and Germany. Barnes concluded that unless major political figures, including Donald Trump in his potential second term, radically course-correct and abandon aggressive global interventionism, they will face severe domestic political backlash, cementing an era of Western decline on the global stage.</p>
